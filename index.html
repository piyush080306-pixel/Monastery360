📋 Before You Go

🪪
Inner Line Permit (ILP)

Required for Indian nationals. Apply online or at Rangpo/Siliguri. Carry ID proof.

📅
Best time

Mar–May & Sep–Nov for clear skies. Monsoons (Jun–Aug) can cause landslides.

🎒
Pack

Layers, rain jacket, comfortable shoes, sunscreen. Dress modestly at monasteries.
